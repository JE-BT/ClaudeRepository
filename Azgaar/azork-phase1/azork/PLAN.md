# azork plan

Turn an Azgaar Fantasy Map Generator world into a Zork-style text adventure that follows one
of the world's journeys. Built in chat sessions with Claude; this repository is the source of
truth for code, worlds, ledgers and content packs.

## Decisions (2026-10-03)

1. **Playable area: the journey corridor.** Only places near a journey's track are generated.
2. **Text: data templates plus content packs.** Each world has `Azgaar/<World>/content/`, written
   in sessions and tagged canon or draft. Content packs are per world.
3. **Goal: the journey.** Complete the journey's stages; the plan is the clock and the stakes.
   Treasure (Zork-style) and knowledge scores may be added later as subsystems.
4. **Terminal first.** Browser and Inform 7 exports later.
5. **Ledger export on exit: keep, discard or fork.**
   - keep: append the session's proposed entries (status draft) to `<World>_ledger.md`;
   - discard: write nothing to the ledger (the transcript is still saved);
   - fork: write `<World>_ledger.<branch>.md` (main ledger plus the session), and allow later
     play to run on that branch.
6. **The master ledger lives in the repo** (`Azgaar/<World>/<World>_ledger.md`).
7. **Clock: dawn-based, simplified.** Declination from day of year, hour angle from latitude,
   local solar time. Start day comes from `content/world.json` (default: spring equinox).
   Stages under 24 h a day depart at dawn and spread their travel hours over daylight, so a
   full day ends at dusk; 24-hour modes never stop.
8. **Plan versus play.** The journey file is the plan and is never rewritten. Play may diverge.
   Faster modes from `transports_allowed` are offered where they fit; difficulty scales with
   the share of plan time saved (light < 25 %, moderate < 50 %, hard < 75 %, severe).
   Example: Lania stage 9 by rowboat is 146 days; a sailing boat saves 81 days (hard), a
   sailing ship 127 days (severe).
9. **Strict initial setup, expansion on demand.** Before a story is generated, every required
   file must be present: stops (stage endpoints), canon places (ledger anchors inside the
   corridor) and sites on the track with a Watabou generator (dungeons). Everything else in
   the corridor is described from map data while passing; entering it pauses play with a
   request for its file (`setup.expansion_message`), then the area is generated.
10. **Dwellings are generated by the engine.** A Dwellings export is used when one has been
    assigned; it is never required.

## File conventions

- Town or village plan: `<burg>.json` (City or Village Generator export).
- Dwelling: `<burg>--<label>.json`, or placed by `content/manifest.json`, or by a ledger entry
  anchored to `building N in <town>.json` and naming the dwelling file.
- Dungeon: `dungeon-<marker id>.json`, or placed by `content/manifest.json`
  (One Page Dungeon exports carry no seed, so the file cannot be matched by content).

## Content pack (draft spec)

`Azgaar/<World>/content/world.json`:
`start_day_of_year`, `strict_mi` (default 2), `corridor_mi` (default 40), `transports_allowed`.

`Azgaar/<World>/content/manifest.json`: `files` → `{filename: {burg, building, marker, label, source}}`.

To come (phase 5): places, people, hooks and encounters, each with an anchor (as in the ledger)
and a status (canon or draft).

## Open items

- One Page Dungeon door type codes: only 3 is known (doors to the outside). 0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 9
  are kept as raw codes.
- Watabou links: a cell's haven and island status are approximated from the cells GeoJSON, which
  only affects coastal City (`sea`) and Village (`estuary`, `island`) parameters.
- The plan clock's journey day 13 for Bayfshear and walking day 9 for Birad match the ledger;
  the ledger's "after dark" Kinargzig crossing is 0.6 mi past the plan's end of walking day 8.
- Repository housekeeping: the Adrer emblem filename begins with a space; Cratay has no cells
  GeoJSON; the Presia ledger is still only in the Claude Project.
