## Session 1 (2026-09-27): the slave trade

### The slave trade — other
- Anchored to: good 31 (Slaves); market-to-market deals in the .map; source cells 5401, 2403, 995, 1005, 1140, 2418, 1102, 781, 5362 and burg 493 Alluma
- Status: canon
- Connections: The carrier houses; Pres; Cleau; The Wildlanders
- Slaves imported by a market mostly go to buyers the model does not record (estates, galleys, workshops)
- Hiring slaves out from markets to burgs, and temple or crown ownership, are secondary arrangements that depend on local culture
- Coastal raiding is the main source of slaves. Many northern source cells lie next to Wildlander cells, so native peoples are the main victims there
- Where the victims are not outsiders, penal servitude (as in the south) or war used as an excuse supplies slaves
- Routing is least-time, with no seasons for now
- Open: why Alluma, Padamia's slave port, lies about 309 mi from the war front

### The carrier houses — other
- Anchored to: cross-enemy deals (e.g. Ruela → Ingnyronte, burg 5 → market at burg 531)
- Status: canon
- Connections: The slave trade; The scattered imperial fleets
- Neutral carrier houses are the main way goods move between enemies, which makes slave traders a power of their own
- Smuggling and pre-war contracts are minor exceptions
- A few carriers keep their own charts of the world as a trade secret (a rare exception to the common belief about Totford)

## Session 2 (2026-09-28): history and the old empire

### Presia — world
- Anchored to: map settings (`lore.name`, calendar "Lympsford Era"); whole-globe extent (360° by 180°)
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Empire of Presia; The Principality of Presia
- The world is called Presia after the fallen Empire of Presia
- The world is a small globe: the equator is about 5,760 mi (data), and a sailing ship could circle it in about 24 days
- Kingdon's west coast and Salmedon's east coast face each other across the map edge, about 770 mi apart (data): the outer crossing

### The Empire of Presia — other (polity)
- Anchored to: burg 2 Pres; the Lympsford Era calendar
- Status: canon
- Connections: Lympsford; The fall of 435; Imperial roles of today's realms; The Angshire; The Wildlanders
- A large, multicultural empire, ruled from Pres, that set the world order and the calendar
- Founded in year 0 at Lympsford by settlers from the Presian north-west (Luari, Shwazen, Norse)
- The empire's language was the Angshire tongue, born on Sunlit; it named the continents, long rivers and open-sea lanes (English-type names on Kingdon, Salmedon, 33 rivers and 22 sea lanes, per the name classifier)
- Member realms fought one another inside the order; the wars recorded as "Rebellion", "Intervention" and "Crusade" read as imperial conflicts
- Fell in 435

### Lympsford — place/event
- Anchored to: province 147 Tinor (Lydonland), the Angshire hearth at cell 4457
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Empire of Presia; The Angshire; Lydonland
- A ford in Tinor where settlers from the Presian north-west landed and the founding compact was sworn in year 0
- No burg of that name exists today

### The fall of 435 — event
- Anchored to: the 14 campaigns begun in 435; unit foundations of 435–439
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Empire of Presia; The Presian War of 435–436; The scattered imperial fleets; Lydonland
- The fall had many causes, and all the arguments stay open. Among them: extinction of the crown's line, federates breaking their oaths, plague (the Pink Fever as a trace), and many others
- In 435, realms that had relied on the imperial order had to raise armies of their own (Lydonland all 5 units, Piereia 5 of 6)

### Imperial roles of today's realms — other
- Anchored to: states 1–19 and neutral lands; companion map
- Status: canon (as drawn on the companion map)
- Connections: The Empire of Presia; The imperial offices
- Crown lands: Presia (the seat) and Piereia (the church province of the crown lands)
- Founding isle and the straits: Lydonland, holding the 25 mi and 123 mi straits (data), with the Erdemdelen bridgehead (province 179, 25 Angshire cells in Esenkoryurt, burg 508 Buckington)
- Federate sword: Esenkoryurt and Beytekyurt (Turchian); Esenkoryurt was sworn in at the founding. Their war since 437 is over the federate inheritance
- Imperial offices: Lalylisia (Exarchate), Ecelia (Eparchy), Bhryreria (Patriarchate)
- Western rim: Padamia, Alverenia, Estrielas, the Astellian coast of the dark cult, policed by the crown
- Client realms of Salmedon: Casteleria, Sampi, Arvicola, Barolia, Tiszalgaria, Rocano, Viroval. Arvicola, styled "Empire" (data), is the eastern claimant to the crown. Viroval is the straits' merchant cities, turned republic
- Outside the order: Erantzia (Euskati), target of six crusades; now the largest army, 22,221 troops (data)
- Wildlander frontier: the unclaimed polar margins

### The imperial offices — belief/other
- Anchored to: state forms of Lalylisia (14), Ecelia (18), Bhryreria (16); religion 8 Gourm Pantheon (a folk faith, not an organised church, per data)
- Status: canon
- Connections: Imperial roles of today's realms; Lalylisia
- Exarch (viceroy), eparch (prefect) and patriarch were imperial civil offices
- Their religious character is recent: after the fall the office-holders made their titles sacred, turning politics into religion

### The Angshire — other (culture)
- Anchored to: culture 2 Angshire, hearth cell 4457 (Tinor); Lydonland; Erdemdelen (province 179); Reazionia (province 279, 3 cells)
- Status: canon
- Connections: Lympsford; The Wildlanders; Lydonland
- A partly assimilated settler-and-native culture: settlers from the Presian north-west merged with natives of Sunlit related to the Keltan
- Their tongue became the imperial language; it survives as a vernacular only on Sunlit and in its offshoots

### The Wildlanders — other (culture)
- Anchored to: culture 0 (Wildlands), same naming tradition as the Angshire; 40–70°N and 70–80°S; 72 cells in Sampi
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Angshire; The slave trade
- A mix, mostly native peoples who adopted the imperial tongue from frontier outposts, with some descendants of the outposts themselves
- The imperial names on their rivers (Kington, Portston, Watchfield, Axbridge) are the empire's frontier map
- They are the main victims of coastal slave raiding

### Totford Library — place
- Anchored to: marker 48, cell 4324 (Lydonland)
- Status: canon
- Connections: Lydonland; The carrier houses
- Held the empire's archive and world map
- The common belief is that Totford holds the full knowledge of the world, and that only the restorationists can read it

### Lydonland — state (factions)
- Anchored to: state 12; capital burg 12 Lydon; all 5 units founded 435–439 (data)
- Status: canon
- Connections: Lympsford; Totford Library; The scattered imperial fleets
- Divided between restorationists (the calendar, the alliances with six states, Totford) and isolationists (the new fleets and regiments, the straits, the war with Viroval, arms bought from Esenkoryurt)
- The isolationists hold most of the power

### The scattered imperial fleets — event
- Anchored to: naval units in the .map; Presian 1st Fleet (burg 2 Pres, founded 53, 2 ships); Estrielas 1st Fleet (burg 10 Socen, 58 ships)
- Status: canon (dispersal); draft (piracy)
- Connections: The fall of 435; The carrier houses
- After 435 several fleets stayed with their local rulers and many struck out on their own
- Draft: some may have turned to piracy on the western rim; it is too soon to say
- Presia's lack of ships (the oldest fleet in the world, now 2 ships) shows what the years since the fall have done to the old seat

### The Principality of Presia — state
- Anchored to: state 2; capital burg 2 Pres (cell 1445)
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Empire of Presia; The Presian War of 435–436; The Bordieaux reform; Fercelles; Cleau
- The remnant of the imperial seat
- Its wars before 435 were the crown's wars
- Its Long Quiet (300–434) left it rich (treasury 815), lightly taxed (sales tax 0.11) and nearly unarmed (2,366 troops) (data)
- Broke its defence pact with Beytekyurt in 437 for fear of Esenkoryurt (data)

### The Presian War of 435–436 — event
- Anchored to: campaign "Presian War" (Piereia against Presia, 435–436)
- Status: canon (outcome); draft (motive)
- Connections: Piereia; Arms sales to Piereia
- Presia lost and paid reparations in arms made in Cleau
- Draft (leaning): Piereia's aim was a raid to pay for its newly raised army; custody of Fercelles remains possible as a later ambition

### Arms sales to Piereia — other
- Anchored to: market at burg 725 Cleau → Piereian markets (arms and artillery worth 181, the largest market-to-market flow in the world, per data)
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Presian War of 435–436; Cleau
- The sales began as reparations and continued as deliberate buffer policy against Padamia

### The Bordieaux reform — belief
- Anchored to: religion 17 Bordieaux Pantheon (organised; origins Fercellesism and Deities of the Enlightened Being; one cell, Pres)
- Status: canon
- Connections: Pres; Fercelles; Cleau; The slave trade
- A new reform movement founded in Pres after 435
- Founded by Luari priests who broke with the Fercelles hierarchy
- Its doctrine borrows from the Shwazen faith, so the orthodox see it as heresy
- The crown market at Pres forbids slave sales, and this causes friction

### Pres — burg
- Anchored to: burg 2, cell 1445
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Principality of Presia; The Bordieaux reform
- The old imperial capital, still the second city of the world (43.8k, data)
- The crown market forbids slave sales

### Fercelles — burg
- Anchored to: burg 743, cell 1809 (Fercellesism hearth); province 30 Fercelles Margrave
- Status: canon
- Connections: The Bordieaux reform; Piereia
- The holy town of Fercellesism
- The margrave and clergy are hostile to the court
- Lies 285 mi from Pres but 172 mi from Piereia's Ingnyronte (burg 531) (data)

### Cleau — burg
- Anchored to: burg 725, cell 2737
- Status: canon
- Connections: Arms sales to Piereia; The slave trade
- Presia's arms town, which grew during the Long Quiet and made the reparation arms
- Its buyers import dear-tier slaves from Ruela in defiance of the crown's ban

### The Presian Rebellion (179–180) — event
- Anchored to: campaign "Presian Rebellion" (Lalylisia against Presia)
- Status: draft
- Connections: Lalylisia; Ebruckia
- Leaning: the Exarch of Lalylisia claimed Ebruckia (province 33, the Shwazen march; Shwazen hearth at cell 1576)
- The alternative, overlordship of all Presia, is not rejected

## Contradictions and open items
- No contradictions between new material and the slave-trade canon. That canon called the Wildlanders native peoples, and the accepted "mix, mostly natives" reading keeps it.
- Presia's pre-435 wars are read as the crown's own wars, including wars on members such as Lalylisia (53) and Piereia (298). This fits a feuding imperial order but should be kept in mind.
- Open from the slave-trade session: what the Alluma trade consists of, whether slave status can change, and who the dear slaves are for.
- No journeys have been explored yet (The Sworn Airship: Pulum → Vepara → Ayruk → Pres → Duzunte → Balmastarc), so there is no plan-versus-record slippage to report.
