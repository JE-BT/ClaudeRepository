# Roadmap

azrpg is in alpha. These are the directions planned for the next versions. Each is designed on
paper before it is built.

## Alpha 3

**Funding a long journey.** Long journeys cost more than a single traveller can carry. Planned ways
to pay for them, each drawn from the map:

- a company purse, pooled at the Gathering and backed according to the journey's type;
- a patron's writ from a ruler, temple or guild, honoured in friendly states and dangerous in
  hostile ones, with real political stakes;
- a prologue of paid work before setting out;
- credit from merchants and temples, repayable at the destination;
- difficulty levels that set how much money there is.

**Charts.** Every place carries the date of the last thing you learned about it, from what you
saw, the news you heard, or a chart. Charts are bought at libraries and harbours, are dated when
drawn, and can be traded with captains; some are wrong. Lenses show your chart; Sandbox adds a
view of the true world.

**Traffic.** The ships, coaches and wagons of every passenger line are drawn where their timetables
put them, and can be hailed for their timetables and news; a density limit and a switch keep the
map readable.

## Beta

**Land, control and contestation.** Each place will carry four layers: who claims it, who actually
controls it, whom its people identify with, and who else recognises the claim. Contestation arises
where these differ: frontiers, occupation, peoples without a polity, rival claims. Polities other
than states (leagues of towns, temple lands, nomadic confederacies, village self-rule) form and
fade where people, surplus and a defensible centre come together. Borders move through this
process.

**A growing network.** Passenger lines open where trade grows and after peace, and close when
unused.

**A record of play.** Export a journey's record: route, events and what changed in the world.

## Release

Performance on large maps, balance across many worlds, saves that carry across versions, and full
documentation for map makers.
