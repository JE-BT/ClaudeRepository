The rules are simple. Each player is a merchant looking for commissions. Commissions are given by states, and each state has a custom deck of commissions. These are created from the inter-market trade deals in the Azgaar export, and the custom deck is created from the deals from every market in the state. Two copies of each deal are made: one for outgoing market and one for incoming market.

A commission is completed by building infrastructure at every station along a continuous route between the markets. All players who control stations along this route split the income from the commission according to the control track on the commission card. The game ends when any player reaches 50 total income from their commissions, before expenses. If the player runs out of money, they lose. The 50 goal is subject to change for balance.

The game has a set of components:
50 stations in each player color (up to 6 players).
Infrastructure in three types: land (green), sea (blue), and air (white).
Unlimited tokens in each player color.
Event stars in each infrastructure color (green, blue, and white).
An unlimited coin supply.
A map board. The map board shows the states, the markets, event locations (markers), the roads (land), sea routes (sea) and where air infrastructure can be built (taken from airship journeys in text, if any, and teleport markers, if any). Bergs over a certain size and importance threshold (population, capital cities, forts) are marked as 'stops' and all stops must have a station and infrastructure present for a route to be formed. All markers are snapped to the nearest route, given the color of the route type (sea or land). Air events are used when the marker is too far from the route. The map also identifies places for neutral infrastructure to be placed (the biggest cities in the world). 
Infrastructure cards in four types: land, sea, air, and wild.
Commission cards which list the state it is from, the good being traded, the distance of the trade, how income is divided, and a space to hold event stars from the route.
Event cards which provide story for what happens to ships, wagons, or airships along the route, and can be negative or positive. They are taken from the markers in Azgaar's export. All event cards have event text for all three types of infrastructures and the player must choose based on the stars in their commissions.
A budget board to track expenses (station cost and infrastructure) and income (from commissions). It has placed on the board for each station, with a +1 upkeep cost revealed every 5 stations. Around the edge are two tracks going from 1 to 50. Every time the player builds infrastructure, they move a player token up the red expense track, 1 for every land, 2 for every sea, and 5 for every air they build. Every time the player completes a commission, they move their player marker up the income track the number of spaces indicated on the commission card after allocating the income.

These components are used to complete commissions. A commission is completed by getting a station in every stop along the chosen path between markets. This trade route can be made of connected stations of any color with the appropriate infrastructure. Roads from a station can only be used if land infrastructure is present, sea routes can only be used if sea infrastructure is present, and all air infrastructures connect but can only be built in marked stations. 

The game is set up by placing the map in the middle of the table with three stacks of infrastructure tiles next to it, one in each color. The infrastructure deck goes next to this, with the drop three drawn and placed next to the board. Along the top of the board go the custom commission decks, one for each state, representing the market deals in that state. Some stops on the map are marked with black crosses, and these receive neutral stations with infrastructure for all the routes that connect to it. All players take the stations and player tokens of their color, and their own personal budget board. They start with 10 gold.

Each round goes as follows: 
Before the round begins, all players are allowed to draw three cards from their choice of commission deck (one for each state). They choose one card and put the others on the bottom.
They then draw event cards if they have any completed commissions with event stars on them, one card per star. They must then assign all of their event stars to different cards and read the event of the matching color, following its instructions. After all events resolve, event stars are returned to the commission cards.
Then, each player does the following on their turn:
1 - Place or removes a station of their color. If a station is removed, they must remove all infrastructure from that stop. They then move their player token up or down the station upkeep tracker and the infrastructure expense tracker on the budget board as necessary.
2 - Draw the top card of the infrastructure deck, or take one of the three face up cards, replacing it.
3 - Play as many infrastructure cards as desired. Infrastructure can only be built at a station the player controls and the route of the appropriate type must be present. They then move their token up the infrastructure expense track according to the rules of the budget board.
4 - Declare any complete commissions, placing them face up on the table. They may also withdraw from a commission, discarding it. They then demonstrate a valid route for each commission, even ones played on previous turns. If any commission does not have a valid route, it must be discarded. Every turn, the commission card is rebalanced to match a working route, moving player tokens on the station control track on each commission board and placing any event stars in the event space on the commission card that the route passes through. 
5 - Add up the upkeep, infrastructure expense, and income on the budget board, taking the appropriate amount of gold.

A player can only hold three commissions at a time. Any commission over three means they must immediately discard one. Failing to find a valid route in step 4 of the turn also causes the commission to be discarded. All discarded commissions cause a player to draw one less card when drawing commissions. 

The budget boards contain asymmetric bonuses for each player, with bonuses given for specific goods, discounts for infrastructure, and a card bonus for a home state.
